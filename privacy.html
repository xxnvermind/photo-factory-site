Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PhotoFactory ("we", "our", or "the Service") handles information when users access or use PhotoFactory. PhotoFactory is a content preparation tool designed to help creators organize product assets and prepare photo carousel content.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used and the application's actual configuration, PhotoFactory may process the following information:

  • Account and contact information that a user voluntarily provides.
  • Product images, product descriptions, captions, and other content uploaded or submitted by the user.
  • TikTok account identifiers and authorization information when a user chooses to connect a TikTok account.
  • Content publishing status and technical logs needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

We aim to collect only information necessary to provide the features requested by the user. The actual information processed depends on which features are implemented and enabled.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Prepare, organize, and review product photo content.
  • Provide requested TikTok account connection and content upload features, when available and authorized.
  • Display content processing and publishing status.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Respond to user requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. TikTok Account Authorization

If TikTok integration is enabled, users may be redirected to TikTok to review and authorize requested permissions. PhotoFactory will use authorized access only for the features and purposes disclosed to the user and permitted by TikTok.

Users can decline authorization and can revoke the application's access through the applicable TikTok account or application settings. Revocation may prevent connected features from working.

4. Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Access tokens, secrets, and other authentication credentials must be protected and must not be exposed in public repositories, client-side code, or public logs.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate enabled features, such as hosting, storage, or authorized AI services. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the Service.

We do not sell users' personal information. Any third-party processing must be evaluated against the actual services used and applicable privacy requirements.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal and operational requirements. Users may request access to or deletion of their information using the contact details provided by the operator of PhotoFactory.

7. Children's Privacy

PhotoFactory is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the Service under applicable laws and platform requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Service or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
